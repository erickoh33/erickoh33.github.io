How this is calculated

This uses a common needs-based rule of thumb:

  1. Life need = annual income × years to replace + outstanding debts + children's education.
  2. Life gap = life need − existing life cover − savings you could use.
  3. CI need = annual income × years of income for CI (many planners suggest a few years' income to cover treatment and time off work).
  4. CI gap = CI need − existing CI cover.

It doesn't adjust for inflation, investment returns or your spouse's income.

How this is calculated
  1. Your monthly expenses are grown by inflation until retirement.
  2. Amount needed is the lump sum that can pay those expenses each year for your years in retirement, rising with inflation, while the balance keeps earning the expected return.
  3. Projected savings = current savings and monthly contributions grown at the expected return until retirement.
  4. Extra monthly saving is what you'd need to add each month, at the same return, to close the shortfall.
