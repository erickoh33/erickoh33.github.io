My free calculators give you a quick estimate in a few minutes. They're also a first taste of the needs-based planning my team does with clients every day, and the kind of meaningful conversation you'll learn to lead when you join us.

FAQ

Questions you might have

Do I need sales experience?

No. Many people start with no sales background. You'll get full training and mentorship, and the AI toolkit makes it easier to get started with confidence.

Do I need to be good with tech?

Not at all. If you can use a smartphone, you can learn this. I'll teach you the AI tools step by step. And if you're already tech-savvy, you'll have a head start.

Is it insurance or takaful?

Both. Our team offers conventional insurance and takaful plans, so you can serve every client based on their needs and preferences.

Who can apply?

Malaysians and those eligible to work in Malaysia. We welcome fresh grads, tech-savvy people, and experienced managers with 3+ years of people management in finance, marketing or banking for our Unit Manager role.
