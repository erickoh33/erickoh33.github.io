# Titans Fly the Kuala Lumpur Flag: A National Record and a Medal Rush at the 2026 SEA Finswimming Championship

From 29 to 31 October 2026, the MBJB Aquatic Centre in Johor Bahru hosted the Southeast Asia Finswimming Championship 2026. Running alongside it was the first-ever Southeast Asia Age Group Finswimming Championship. The Malaysia Finswimming Association (MFSA) organised both events, which drew 137 athletes from four nations: Malaysia (61), Vietnam (46), Cambodia (17) and Indonesia (13). They competed across 72 events.

## A Proud Result for Malaysia

Malaysia's finswimmers did the country proud, finishing **second overall** with **46 gold, 54 silver and 57 bronze medals, 157 medals in total**. Vietnam topped the table with 92 gold, 62 silver and 6 bronze (160 medals). Cambodia had its best result yet with 11 gold, 16 silver and 19 bronze.

The racing was fast. Vietnam's Nguyen Thanh Loc, a former world championships bronze medallist, set Southeast Asian records in the men's 50m surface (15.18s) and 100m surface (34.71s). His teammate Nguyen Tien Dat set another in the men's 400m immersion (2:54.37).

This was the first time the region held an age-group championship, so swimmers as young as under-7 got to race internationally. That matters a lot to a kids' club like ours. It shows young Malaysian finswimmers can now race the region's best close to home.

## 11 Titans Representing Kuala Lumpur

Eleven Titans represented **Kuala Lumpur**, seven in the Open Group and four in the Closed Group, and together they won **10 gold, 12 silver and 16 bronze** between them, with **20 personal bests** and a **new national record**.

Our seven Open Group Titans brought home **3 gold, 6 silver and 14 bronze**, plus 8 PBs. (Relay medals shared by several Titans are counted once per relay.)

- **Ehren Ae Chern Koh** (11): **3 relay golds** in the 4x50m SB, Surface and Bifin Mixed Relays, plus silver in the 50m Bifin (27.87) and 50m Surface (27.31), and bronze in the 100m Bifin (1:03.10) and 100m Surface (1:02.40, PB).
- **Shaiful Addin Adinda Shaiful Anuar** (16): national record and bronze in the 200m Surface (1:46.18), plus bronze in the 50m Apnea (20.36), 100m Surface (47.19) and 50m Surface (21.07), and three relay silvers.
- **Azmeer Zuhairi** (16): silver in the 200m Immersion (1:56.42, PB) and bronze in the 400m Immersion (4:19.23, PB).
- **Roxanne Hui Nee Soon** (19): bronze in the 100m Immersion (49.37, PB) and 200m Immersion (1:54.43, PB), plus three relay bronzes.
- **Aidan Ae Bin Koh** (20): bronze in the 100m Bifin (47.23, PB), plus two relay bronzes.
- **Elson Ae Shang Koh** (18): bronze in the 100m Immersion (42.94, PB), a 100m Surface PB (43.37), plus two relay bronzes.
- **Megat Muhammad Malliqie** (18): two relay bronzes (4x50m Surface Mixed, 4x100m Bifin Mixed).

### Kuala Lumpur – Closed Group

Our four Titans racing for Kuala Lumpur in the Closed Group came home with **7 gold, 6 silver and 2 bronze, and 12 personal bests**!

- **Max Mito Lau** (10): **4 golds from 4 races!** Max won the 50m bifin (36.37), 100m bifin (1:24.08), 100m surface and 50m surface (38.47), with 3 PBs. A perfect meet!
- **Ansel Koh Ae Hao** (12): **2 gold, 2 silver.** He won the 200m and 400m bifin (5:02.31) and took silver in the 50m bifin (27.16) and 100m bifin (1:01.17), with 3 PBs.
- **Thaanyaa Sasee** (11): **1 gold, 2 silver, 1 bronze.** She won the 50m bifin in 28.31, took silver in the 100m bifin (1:02.56) and 200m bifin (2:24.58), and bronze in the 50m surface, with 3 PBs.
